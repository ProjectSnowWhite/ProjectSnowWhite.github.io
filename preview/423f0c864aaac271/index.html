    What it is: The full 300-frame clip as one stream of 6.7 Mbit/s, half the cameras' resolution, five 2-second pieces with the still room held across them.     New in it: Sharper fine texture than the 'still room' entry below: the training now also optimises for perceived detail, the spare splats go where several cameras agree the picture is wrong, the view through the windows starts from proper far-away points, and the finishing pass runs twice as long. Perceived error about 9 % lower (LPIPS 0.140 against 0.154), for 0.5 dB less on the pixel-average score - that score prefers a slightly softer picture.
    Look at: Fine texture: the salmon's fibres, the specks in the pan, labels on the shelves, the shirt. The view through the windows. The room at the 2, 4, 6 and 8 second marks (it should stay calm).
    Known issues: Fixed today: the whole-picture flash every 2 seconds was the player (for a frame or two it drew the old and the new piece on top of each other); it now prepares the next piece off-screen and fades over in 0.16 s. The first version of that fix waited too long for the next piece and showed one still frame from 2 seconds on; fixed, and the player now has tests for both. Still known: a faint shift of edges in the room at the hand-over (the pieces are trained apart; a training-side fix is next). Pieces 2 to 5 a little softer than the first 2 seconds. Moving hands and the flame remain the softest parts.
