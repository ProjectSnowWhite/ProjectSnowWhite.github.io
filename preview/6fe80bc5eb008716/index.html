How to use the slate

  1. Turn the screen's brightness up, Night Shift / True Tone off.
  2. At the start and at the end of the take, hold the screen toward each camera for two seconds, in the scene's light - near enough that it fills a tenth of the picture or more - and move it slowly up and down through the picture once.
  3. The chirps are on: leave them on while you show the slate (Sound switches them off).
  4. Board, once a rig: before or after the take, move it slowly through each camera's whole picture - the corners too - and tilt it. Slate goes back.
