Updated 25 October 2026, 13:10 - motion and face together. Test builds of the phone as a window into the clips: work in progress, for an iPhone (Safari).

  • Test page: the phone as a window (a plain room)
    What it is: A simple 3D room to test the window effect on its own, without a clip: the phone's motion sensors and, if allowed, the front camera follow your head.
    New in it: Tilting now turns the view like a camera (before: inverted, as if orbiting a point in the distance). The room is framed by the screen's long side in both orientations; the controls start folded behind the ☰ button; a double tap recentres.
    Look at: Tap start, allow motion and the camera. Turn the phone: the view should turn with it, the way a camera would (right edge away looks left, top toward you looks up), up to about 20°, drifting back to centre over a few seconds. Then move your head: the room shifts as if through a window.
    Known issues: Second build: the turn is checked on a desktop with simulated sensors, not yet on an iPhone.
  • Flame salmon - the phone as a window (motion and face)
    What it is: The 10-second flame salmon clip (5 pieces, 9.3 Mbit/s) in the cube player, the view following your head: the motion sensors between camera frames, the front camera's view of your face for the position.
    New in it: Motion and face now work together: turning the phone turns the view exactly as with motion only (a still head no longer cancels it), and moving your head adds the window's parallax inside the turned view. Tapping 'Immersion' again recentres both.
    Look at: Tap 'Immersion' in the bar and allow motion and the camera; tap it again to recentre (where you hold the phone now). The read-out (Stats) shows what drives the view: the face or the sensors, the tracking rate, the eye's position.
    Known issues: Third build: checked with simulated sensors, not yet on an iPhone. Moving far off the cameras' line shows veils on moving parts (being worked on).
  • Flame salmon - motion sensors only
    What it is: The same clip, the view moved by tilting the phone only - no camera asked for.
    New in it: Tilting turns the view like a camera (before: inverted, orbiting a distant point).
    Look at: Tap 'Immersion', allow motion, turn the phone a little: the view turns with it, the way a camera would.
    Known issues: Second build.
  • Cook spinach - the phone as a window (motion and face)
    What it is: The 10-second cook spinach clip in the cube player, the view following your head.
    New in it: As above: motion and face together.
    Look at: As above.
    Known issues: Third build. This clip still has the veil over the dog and a ghost by the chef's hand late in the clip (fix in training).

How to look: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom; the bar at the bottom has play / pause, the timeline, the original camera views and the render size.
