The phone is a window. A room lies behind the glass: turning the phone turns the view, like a camera; moving your head shows the room from another angle, as through real glass. Hold the phone as you normally do, about 35 cm away, and tap Start.

Tilt uses the motion sensors only. Face uses the front camera (the picture stays on the phone, nothing is sent). Fused uses both. Safari asks for each. A double tap on the picture recentres; the button in the corner shows the controls.
